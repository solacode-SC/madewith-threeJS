# Lumina & the Azure Steps (`blue-medina-road`)

An interactive 3D fantasy Three.js / React Three Fiber world inspired by the painted cobalt stairways of Chefchaouen and a levitating chibi sky-wanderer.

## Highlights

- **90-Meter Winding & Ascending Blue Medina Road**:
  - **136 Impasto Cobalt Steps** with palette-knife oil textures and authentic whitewash paint drips.
  - **5 Discoverable Landmarks**:
    1. **The Painter's Steps** — Recreates the foreground white amphora flower pots, overhanging cedar balcony planters with trailing ivy, wrought-iron windows, and the tall white chimney tower at the top of the stairs.
    2. **Whispering Lantern Arch** — Moroccan keyhole horseshoe arches with Zellij geometric mosaic spandrels and suspended star lanterns.
    3. **Courtyard of the Sky Fountain** — Open sunlit plaza centered around a levitating turquoise water orb and octagonal mosaic basin.
    4. **Bridge of Levitating Amphoras** — High-altitude sapphire stairway flanked by floating ceramic vases and drifting cloud banks.
    5. **Celestial Belvedere** — Domed Moroccan summit pavilion crowning the end of the long road.
- **Flying Chibi Character (`FlyingChibiGirl`)**:
  - Sculpted with short dark-brown bob hair, bouncy *ahoge* top curl, chubby rosy cheeks with diagonal blush lines, serene closed eyes, floating 4-pointed star sparkle (`✧`), oversized sage-grey tunic, coral-pink backpack, wide lavender pants, dangling bare feet, and sheathed katana.
  - Continuous mid-air levitation, aerodynamic pitch/roll banking, streaming wind ribbons, and a 4-pointed star sparkle trail.
- **15 Collectible Lumina Sky Stars (`✧`)** & **3 Atmosphere Modes** (`☀️ Chefchaouen Noon`, `🌅 Golden Medina`, `✨ Starlit Fantasy`).

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys** (or On-Screen Flight Pad): Fly forward, backward, left, and right along the winding road
- **Shift**: Sky Boost
- **Space / E**: Soar higher above the steps
- **C / Q**: Swoop lower near the steps
- **Click Any Step / Landmark Pill**: Automatically fly to that point along the road
- **Mouse Drag / Scroll**: 360° Orbit & Zoom around the character
