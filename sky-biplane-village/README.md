# Sora & the Meadow Sky Biplane (`sky-biplane-village`)

An interactive hand-drawn 3D sky & pastoral village flight world built with **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber**, **Custom GLSL Gouache & Watercolor Shaders**, and **Inverted-Hull Ink Outlines**.

## Visual & Interactive Highlights
- **Hand-Drawn Vintage Biplane (`Sora`)**: Cambered cream-linen wings with painted rib shading and dark ink outlines, interplane N-struts & rigging wires, dark olive-charcoal nose cowling with white radiator face, spinning wooden propeller with motion-blur disc, open cockpit pilot Sora with brass aviator goggles and fluttering silk scarf, dynamic white contrail ribbon, and a **crisp dark-green ground-projected silhouette shadow** gliding across the terrain below.
- **Hand-Inked & Watercolor Fenced Village Below**: 10 fenced pastoral homestead plots with 2-rail wooden fences & open farm gates, rustic timber cottages with 4 pitched shingle roof palettes (`teal-slate`, `cedar-umber`, `blue-slate`, `moss-timber`), cream bargeboard trim, red-brick chimneys with animated rising smoke wisps, companion barn sheds, utility poles, Brookside Watermill, Hilltop Windmill, and Meadow Airstrip Windsock.
- **Custom GLSL Gouache Terrain & Morning Sunbeams**: Multi-layered diagonal gouache brushstrokes, procedural hand-inked grass tuft hatching (`||/`), diagonal golden morning sunbeam shafts, and drifting low morning mist wisps.
- **5 Camera Angles & 3 Pastoral Flight Songs**: Switch between `Sky Illustration`, `Village Overlook`, `Wing Chase`, `Wingtip Cinema`, and `Cockpit POV` while listening to 3 Web Audio API pastoral flight songs.
