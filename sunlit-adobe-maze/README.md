# Mina & the Sunlit Mashrabiya Labyrinth (`sunlit-adobe-maze`)

An interactive 3D watercolor-inspired Adobe Archway & Mashrabiya Lattice Window Maze built with **React Three Fiber**, **Three.js**, and **PostProcessing**.

## Highlights

- **Roofed Timber-Beam & Adobe Archway Architecture (No Sky)**:
  - Completely enclosed beneath weathered cedar rafters and wooden ceiling planks.
  - Hand-troweled ochre mud-plaster walls, pointed Persian/Islamic archways, stepped window corbels, woven kilim carpets with 3D fringes, lathe-turned ribbed terracotta water jars, and hanging pierced-brass lanterns.
- **Mashrabiya Lattice Sunlight Projections**:
  - Intricate 4-tier carved wooden lattice window screens casting dappled geometric diamond sunlight across the recessed window sills, adjacent plaster walls, and woven rugs.
- **Mina (Red-Coat Chibi Explorer)**:
  - Sculpted 3D character matching Reference Image 2: glossy jet-black bob hair with bouncy left-side hair loops, happy closed arc eyes (`^ ^`), round crimson cheeks, bright red A-line coat with 7 white buttons, black boots, and cheerful waving animation.
- **Expert Game-Dev Camera & Movement**:
  - **3rd-Person Comfort Follow (`follow`)**: Wall-collision spring-arm camera that smoothly follows Mina down corridors without ever clipping through walls or ceiling beams.
  - **1st-Person Character POV (`pov`)**: True eye-level perspective looking directly through Mina's eyes with natural walking head-bob.
  - **Watercolor Archway Angle (`painting`)**: Frames corridors in the exact architectural composition of Reference Image 1.
  - **Iterative Circle-vs-AABB Wall Sliding + BFS Maze Pathfinder**: Buttery-smooth movement (`WASD / Arrows`), click-to-walk, interactive 7×7 parchment floorplan mini-map, and a dynamic Golden Sun-Thread guide.
