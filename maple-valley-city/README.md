# Kaede & the Maple Brush Valley (`maple-valley-city`)

A sprawling 11×11 watercolor & sumi-e brushwork mountain village city inspired by East Asian *shanshui* painting.

## Highlights
- **Painterly Brushwork & Smooth Organic Sculpting**: Catenary-curved slate-tiled roofs, spline-curved autumn maple & ancient pine trunks, sculpted river boulders, and custom GLSL domain-warped watercolor brush clouds.
- **Discover From Roads & From Up Above**: Explore along winding sandy brush roads (`Road View`, `Painting Vista`, `First-Person POV`) or switch to **`From Up (Sky View)`** and **`Cloud Orbit`** to admire the entire city from above and click any road to navigate there.
- **Studio Web Audio Soundtrack**: 3 selectable full-length polyphonic East Asian / Ghibli-inspired songs (*Maple Breeze*, *Cloud Brush Valley*, *Mountain Road Lanterns*) with stereo convolution hall reverb.
- **Minimal Uncluttered HUD**: Streamlined floating rice-paper pills that keep 96% of the artwork canvas unobstructed.
